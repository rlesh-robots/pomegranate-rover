barrel of water pipeline analogy. (battery-wire)

Electricity	| Water
--- | ---
Volts |	Pressure
Amps |	Flow rate (liters/second)
Wire thickness | Pipe width
Watts (V × A) |	Power: how hard the water turns the wheel
Amp-hours |	How much water is in the barrel
Watt-hours (V × Ah) | How much work the water in the barrel can do
